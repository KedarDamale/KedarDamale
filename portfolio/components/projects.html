Chessablanka

A camera-ready route from a board photograph to an engine-analyzed chess position.

  • Reading a position from a photo meant solving board detection, grid mapping, and piece recognition together.
  • Built an end-to-end YOLOv8 pipeline with a custom dataset and connected detected positions to Stockfish.
  • Reached 98.57% detection accuracy across 500+ test images and returned best-move analysis.

NeuroTRIBE

Reproducible cortical-response analysis for an ADHD research question.

  • Explored whether movie-evoked cortical response patterns differ for participants with confirmed ADHD and a matched comparison group.
  • Built a TRIBE v2 workflow over Healthy Brain Network movie-fMRI data, orchestrated with Docker Compose.
  • Made cohort-level exploratory analysis repeatable, without bundling legally gated inputs or implying clinical diagnosis.

Cattle Monitoring

A field-to-dashboard system for understanding free-grazing cattle movement.

  • Free-grazing herds are difficult to monitor through manual rounds alone.
  • Led a team building ESP32 master-and-node telemetry with GPS/RSSI, a Flask and MongoDB backend, and a live dashboard.
  • Used DBSCAN clustering to surface grazing zones and make movement easier to review in one place.
