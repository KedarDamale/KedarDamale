\sectiontitle{PROJECTS}

\entryline{1. Chessablanka: Chess Position Analyzer}{\myhref{https://github.com/KedarDamale/Chessablanka}{GitHub Link}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a computer-vision tool that converts chessboard photos into playable positions, eliminating manual board transcription for chess analysis and college competition preparation.
  \item Trained a custom YOLOv8 model using an augmented dataset of 1,700+ images to detect chess pieces and reconstruct board positions from photos.
  \item Achieved \textbf{98.57\%} detection accuracy across 500+ test images and integrated Stockfish for position evaluation and best-move recommendations; used by the college IT chess team for competition preparation.
\end{itemize}

\entryline{2. NeuroTRIBE: Brain Activity Analysis for ADHD Research}{\myhref{https://github.com/KedarDamale/NeuroTribe}{GitHub Link}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Developed a neuroimaging research pipeline to investigate differences in brain responses to movie stimuli between participants with and without ADHD.
  \item Automated the analysis of fMRI data using pretrained brain-activity models to identify patterns across brain regions and networks.
  \item Applied statistical testing with confounding-factor adjustments and generated 3D brain visualizations and reports to support research analysis.
\end{itemize}

\entryspace
\entryline{3. CattleMO: Low-Cost Cattle Monitoring with ESP32 and DBSCAN}{\myhref{https://github.com/KedarDamale/ESP32-DBSCAN-Cattle-Monitoring-System}{GitHub Link}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Designed a low-cost cattle monitoring system that reduces dependence on individual GPS trackers by estimating animal proximity through wireless signal strength.
  \item Led development of an ESP32-based tracking prototype with a GPS-equipped master node, wireless child nodes, and a live dashboard for monitoring herd movement.
  \item Applied DBSCAN clustering to identify grazing zones and movement patterns, with telemetry processed through Flask and MongoDB and displayed in a Next.js dashboard.
\end{itemize}
