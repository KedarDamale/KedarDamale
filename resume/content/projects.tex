\entryline{1. Chessablanka: Chess Position Analyzer}{\myhref{https://github.com/KedarDamale/Chessablanka}{GitHub Link}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item No readily available solution could analyze a chess position directly from a board photo, forcing players, including the college IT chess team, to manually transcribe positions into Stockfish during competition preparation.
  \item Built an OpenCV computer-vision pipeline to detect chessboards and map their grids, training a custom YOLOv8 model on Roboflow with an augmented dataset expanded from 700+ to 1,700 images.
  \item Delivered a photo-to-analysis tool used by the IT chess team for college competition preparation, achieving 98.57\% detection accuracy across 500+ test images and integrating Stockfish for position evaluation and best-move analysis.
\end{itemize}

\entryline{2. NeuroTRIBE: Cortical Response Analysis for ADHD Research}{\myhref{https://github.com/KedarDamale/NeuroTribe}{GitHub Link}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Addressed the challenge of reproducibly testing whether ADHD is associated with altered stimulus-evoked cortical responses, where fMRI-to-movie alignment and access-controlled HBN inputs complicate cohort comparisons.
  \item Built an interactive, Docker Compose-based TRIBE v2/HBN movie-fMRI pipeline aligning observed BOLD signals $y_{v,t}$ with pretrained predictions $\hat{y}_{v,t}$ and comparing confirmed-ADHD with non-ADHD cohorts at vertex, region, and network scales.
  \item Computed residuals $e_{v,t}=y_{v,t}-\hat{y}_{v,t}$, summarized vertex-wise deviation and correlation, and tested group effects with age-, sex-, site-, and motion-adjusted models plus false-discovery-rate (FDR) correction; rendered findings as 3D cortical maps and reports.
\end{itemize}
\entryline{3. Low-Cost Cattle Monitoring with ESP32 and DBSCAN}{\myhref{https://github.com/KedarDamale/ESP32-DBSCAN-Cattle-Monitoring-System}{GitHub Link}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Conventional cattle trackers raise deployment costs by requiring a GPS sensor on every animal, despite cattle naturally moving in groups.
  \item Led a team building a lower-cost group-tracking system, replacing per-animal GPS with one GPS-equipped ESP32 master and RSSI-based child nodes to infer relative positions; streamed telemetry to Flask/MongoDB and a live Next.js dashboard.
  \item Applied DBSCAN to RSSI-informed location points, where $N_{\varepsilon}(p)=\{q\mid d(p,q)\le\varepsilon\}$ and a core point satisfies $|N_{\varepsilon}(p)|\ge\mathrm{minPts}$, to cluster grazing zones and reveal herd movement patterns.
\end{itemize}
